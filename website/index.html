THE PROBLEM

Why does this exist?

Continuous flooding can create anaerobic conditions associated with methane formation. AWD introduces dry-down periods. The challenge is proving the water regime and translating evidence into a transparent MRV scenario.

💧

Water regime

Make WET, DRYING and REWETTING states visible instead of relying only on logbooks.
